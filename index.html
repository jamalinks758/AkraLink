FAQ

Common Questions. Straight Answers.

Do I need any technical knowledge to get started?
None at all. You send us your logo, photos, business name, and what you sell. We handle the entire design, build, and launch. The only thing you do is share your new link with your customers.
How long does it take to get my website live?
Most sites go live within 48 hours of receiving your content. The faster you send us your information, the faster your site launches. We keep you updated throughout the process via WhatsApp.
Can I cancel my plan at any time?
Yes. No long-term contracts. Cancel your subscription at any time with no penalties. Your site stays live until the end of your current billing period. We earn your business every month — not by locking you in.
What if I need to update my prices or menu?
That's what the monthly updates are for. Message us on WhatsApp with your changes and we'll update your site within 1–2 business days. The number of included updates per month depends on your plan.
Do I need to buy a domain name?
The Launch plan uses a free GitHub Pages subdomain. The Link and Grow plans include custom domain connection — you purchase your domain (~$15/year) separately and we handle the full setup for you.
What happens to my site if I cancel?
Your site goes offline at the end of your billing period. We recommend downloading your content before cancelling. You can also contact us to discuss transferring ownership of the site files to you directly.
Can I upgrade my plan as my business grows?
Absolutely. You can upgrade from Launch to Link, or Link to Grow, at any time. Just message us on WhatsApp. We'll apply your upgrade and update your site to reflect the new plan's features within 48 hours.
