A Message from Our Pastor

A church where every
person belongs.

Harbour Light Church was founded on a simple belief — that everyone, regardless of where they've been or where they are, deserves to experience the transforming love of Jesus Christ. For over 25 years, we have been that harbour for families, young people, and individuals across St. Lucia.

Whether you're taking your very first step toward faith, or you're looking for a community to grow deeper with God — there is a place for you here. Come on Sunday. We'll be expecting you.

Our Ministries

There is a place for everyone at Harbour Light. Find the ministry where you belong and where you can serve.

Worship & Music Ministry

Vocalists, musicians, and sound technicians who lead the church in praise every Sunday. Auditions open throughout the year.

Learn More
Children's Ministry

A safe, fun, and faith-filled environment for children from ages 3–12 during every Sunday 10:30 AM service. Trained volunteers every week.

Learn More
Youth Ministry

An energetic community for teens and young adults aged 13–25. Friday night youth services, outings, leadership development, and mentorship.

Learn More
Men's & Women's Fellowship

Dedicated spaces for men and women to gather, share life, study the Word, and encourage one another throughout the month.

Learn More
Community Outreach

Feeding programmes, hospital visits, school partnerships, and community service projects — the hands and feet of the church in St. Lucia.

Learn More
Media & Technology

Video production, live streaming, photography, and social media — helping the church reach St. Lucia and the wider Caribbean online.
