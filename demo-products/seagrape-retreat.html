Our Story

Built for the guest
who wants to feel St. Lucia.

Seagrape Retreat was built by a St. Lucian family who wanted to share what makes this island extraordinary — not through a hotel lobby, but through a real home above the sea. Every room was designed by hand. Every breakfast is cooked fresh each morning.

We take just five rooms at a time because we believe every guest deserves attention, quiet, and space. If you want a poolside resort, this isn't your place. If you want to feel the island the way locals do — this is exactly it.
