Our Story

Born from the
island's heartbeat

"I didn't want to cook what people expected from the Caribbean — I wanted to cook what the Caribbean actually tastes like."
— Chef Maricel Joseph, Founder

Soleil started as a weekend pop-up at Reduit Beach. Seven years later, we're still sourcing from the same local farmers, the same fishermen, the same women making dasheen bread in Dennery. We just have a roof now.

Every dish on our menu has a story. We'll tell you all of them if you ask.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you take reservations?
Yes — message us on WhatsApp to book a table. We recommend booking for Friday and Saturday evenings at least 2 days ahead.
Is there parking available?
Yes, free parking is available at Rodney Bay Marina, directly outside the restaurant.
Do you cater for dietary requirements?
We have vegetarian and vegan options, and can accommodate most allergies with advance notice. Let us know when you book.
Can I order for delivery or pickup?
Pickup orders are welcome — just message us on WhatsApp at least 30 minutes ahead. We do not currently offer delivery.
